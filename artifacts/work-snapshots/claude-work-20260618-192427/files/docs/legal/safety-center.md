# ConnectSphere Safety Center

## Meet Safely

- Meet in public places first.
- Tell someone where you are going.
- Keep early plans low-pressure.
- Leave if anything feels wrong.

## Privacy Controls

Users can choose notification consent, analytics consent, location precision, and account/data deletion options from Settings > Privacy & Safety.

## Blocking

Blocking prevents discovery, profile access, messaging, friend requests, plan invites, plan joins, double-date matching, push notifications, and share-link joins between the two users.

## Reporting

Reports include reason, context, target content, reporter, reported user, timestamps, and moderation status. Urgent safety reports should also be sent to support@connectsphere.app.
