# ConnectSphere Community Guidelines

ConnectSphere should feel safe, respectful, and low-pressure.

## Not Allowed

- Harassment, hate, threats, stalking, coercion, or sexual pressure.
- Impersonation, fake profiles, scams, spam, or misleading invitations.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Evading blocks or encouraging others to contact someone who blocked you.
- Illegal activity, exploitative content, or unsafe meetups.

## Safety Tools

Users can report, block, request account deletion, request a data export, and contact support from Settings or in-context safety surfaces.

Reports are reviewed in a moderation queue and may result in warnings, restrictions, content removal, or account action.
