# ConnectSphere Privacy Policy

ConnectSphere collects the information needed to create an account, build a profile, recommend people or plans, send notifications, operate payments, investigate reports, and keep the service secure.

## Data We Collect

- Account data such as user ID, sign-in method, and session metadata.
- Profile data such as name, bio, photos, interests, location preference, and privacy settings.
- User content such as messages, plans, invites, reports, blocks, and support requests.
- Device and operational data such as push tokens, app version, API errors, crash logs, and abuse logs.
- Payment data such as subscription status and receipt metadata.
- Analytics events only after consent.

## Account Deletion

Users can start deletion in the app from Settings > Privacy & Safety > Delete account. Deletion removes or anonymizes profile data, photos, messages, plans, push tokens, and sessions where supported. Some records may be retained when required for safety investigations, fraud prevention, disputes, tax, legal compliance, or platform obligations.

## Data Export

Users can request a data export in Settings > Privacy & Safety > Request my data. Export requests are queued and fulfilled after identity and safety checks.
